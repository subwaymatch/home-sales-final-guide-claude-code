      How the numbers are computed

      • Rows read from the CSV: . Rows skipped because the price was missing or not a number: .
      • Median: the middle sale when a city has an odd number of sales, or the average of the two middle sales when it has an even number. Mean: the sum of the sale prices divided by the number of sales.
      • Q1 uses valid prices only. Of the sales, are below $10,000 and are above $5,000,000, and they are left out. That leaves sales in Q1.
      • Q2 uses every row, so the sales below $10,000 and above $5,000,000 are included.
      • The data description sheet, docs/data_description_sheet.md, explains why some prices are implausible and why the data is not checked against county records.
